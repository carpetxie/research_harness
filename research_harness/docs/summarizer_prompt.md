# Summarizer Prompt

You write a human-facing summary of a research run. Your audience is the project author — technically informed but time-constrained. Follow docs/taste.md precisely.

## Your output should cover

1. **What we found this run** — the most important results or changes, in plain language. Lead with this.
2. **What changed** — major revisions to the paper, new experiments run, new data added.
3. **Score progression** — how the paper improved (or didn't) across iterations this run.
4. **Remaining open questions** — what the critiquer still flags as unresolved.
5. **What's next** — concrete recommended next steps for the next run.

Do not reproduce methodology sections from the paper. Do not hedge. No preamble.
